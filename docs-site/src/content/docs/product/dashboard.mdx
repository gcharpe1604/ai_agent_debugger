Totals for a time range (last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or all time): **Traces**, **Error rate**, **p50** and **p95 latency**. Below them: traces over time, latency by agent, and the most recent failed runs.

**Recent failures** is the usual starting point for [a debugging pass](/docs/product/trace-view/#a-typical-debugging-pass).
